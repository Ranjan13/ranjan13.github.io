Translating AI research into practical impact

I study how AI systems understand language, reason, and explain their decisions, with applications in banking and sustainability.

My work brings together explainability, language models, and structured knowledge. With academic and industry partners, I develop methods, evaluate their limitations, and explore how they can be used in practice.

I also co-supervise PhD and FYP students and mentor through SIPGA, ARIA, SINGA, and ARAP programmes. For collaborations or student research opportunities, get in touch.

Trustworthy AI

I aim to make AI decisions easier to inspect and trust. My research tests whether explanations reflect a model’s internal computations, examines how safety mechanisms fail, and develops targeted ways to reduce bias.

Finance

I work on making AI useful under the constraints of financial decision-making: noisy data, changing markets, limited compute, and the need for transparent evidence. My research spans efficient forecasting, explainable financial analysis, investment-report generation, and sustainability disclosures.
